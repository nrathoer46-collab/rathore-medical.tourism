Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide medical advice?

No. Rathore Medical Tourism is designed for medical-tourism coordination and travel support. Diagnosis and treatment decisions should be made by licensed healthcare professionals.

Can you arrange hospitals and doctors?

The production version of this website can list your verified partner hospitals and doctors with accurate credentials and appointment information.

Can family members travel with the patient?

Travel packages can include accompanying family members, subject to applicable immigration and travel requirements.

Can I request a treatment cost estimate?

Yes. After the required information is securely shared, an authorised healthcare provider can provide an appropriate estimate.
